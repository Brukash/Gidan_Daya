# REFERENCES

These were read as search summaries on 2026-11-18 (excerpts, not screenshots). Nothing below is a measured value from a live page; treat every observation as **inferred from the published description**.

## 1. OnePangea — https://onepangea.com/

- **Observed (summary):** the marketing page embeds its own management portal under a "Sample data" label, with a KPI strip (members, savings, loans out), an approval queue and a monthly contributions chart.
- **Principle taken:** show the product in the hero, and label sample data explicitly. Directly informs the landing hero's product window and its `Sample data` badge.
- **Things to avoid:** ERP feature-list density; generic "empowering" copy. Our copy stays on the two real jobs (subscribe, administer).

## 2. Eswatini SACCO case study — https://www.lyomu.com/case-studies/eswatini-sacco

- **Observed (summary):** SACCO members self-serve balances, deposits, withdrawals, loan applications and repayments; the case study admits its screens carry placeholder currency and phone-prefix data.
- **Principle taken:** membership comes first — a member's own position is the home screen, not an admin report. Informs the Overview leading with the member's commitments.
- **Things to avoid:** generic neobank visual language. Our surfaces are paper and rules, not gradient card stacks.

## 3. Monarch design specification — https://designmunk.com/monarch

- **Observed (summary):** warm off-white paper base (`#f6f5f3`), deep ink text (`#22201d`), one orange action accent (`#ff692d`), editorial serif headlines at tight negative tracking, modern sans for utility text, 8px spacing scale.
- **Principle taken:** paper ground + ink + exactly one action accent, with serif for editorial voice and sans for dense UI. Informs the palette roles and the Fraunces/Plex division.
- **Things to avoid:** the soft consumer-fintech warmth — we cool the paper slightly and use forest rather than soft ink so the record reads institutional.

## 4. Cooperative Admin — https://saheedibikunle.com/designs

- **Observed (summary):** a permission-layered officer dashboard for contributions, loans, withdrawals and repayments; approval actions require confirmation plus an audit note, so the paper trail is part of the flow.
- **Principle taken:** administrative actions carry their evidence. Informs the secretariat/Applicants surface and the per-application reference numbers shown in tables and exports.
- **Things to avoid:** an officer-only product. The member is the primary audience here; admin surfaces are secondary navigation.
